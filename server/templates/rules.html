Rules

This store is for homebrew: apps, tools, emulators, games and demos made by the community.

Not allowed

Pirated or commercial game dumps, copyrighted content you don’t have the right to share, malware, anything that steals data or damages consoles or accounts, and apps that hide what they do.

Review

Every new app and every new package goes through moderator review before it is public. Moderators may remove anything at any time.

Your responsibility

Creators are responsible for what they upload. Users install at their own risk and should check the SHA-256 shown on each app page.
